The City Science and Complex Systems Lab is organizing, along with other research groups from the LINCOLN and BIOPHYS INFN initiatives, the **2026 edition of the Arcidosso Challenge**, the workshop for young researchers in the fields of Complex Systems, Networks, Nonlinear Dynamics and Applied Statistical Physics in general.

A significant amount of time will be devoted each day to **thematic round tables for young scientists**, in order to foster collaborations and the exchange of knowledge, techniques and ideas.

While the organization is internal to INFN, applications are welcome also from **outside the Institute**.

Please find a more detailed presentation and the subscription form on the [Challenge Home Page](https://agenda.infn.it/event/52100/).

**Subscription deadline:  30/7/2026**

For further information refer to the Home Page or contact emanuele.locatelli@unipd.it , giulio.colombini2@unibo.it or armando.bazzani@unibo.it .
